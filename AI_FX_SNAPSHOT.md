**Snapshot date:** 2026-09-25

These rates are intentionally static for this build. They are not refreshed automatically. Each purchase persists its own `fx_rate_snapshot`, `fx_snapshot_date`, local purchase amount, fixed-profit amount and maturity payout, so later configuration changes cannot alter an existing purchase.
