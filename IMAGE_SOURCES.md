# Image Sources

## Market photography

The public experience uses a curated country-specific photography set from Unsplash. Each photo below was selected from a photo page marked free to use under the Unsplash License. The application uses the Unsplash download endpoint for delivery and does not present these photographs as company logos, endorsements, regulatory images, or evidence of company performance.

The market image is used as contextual photography on the homepage and selected public, authentication and catalogue screens. Company cards continue to use neutral monograms unless separately documented company media is added.

No third-party company logo is automatically seeded or hotlinked. The company_images and image_sources tables remain available for administrator-reviewed company media with source URL, provider, creator, licence, attribution and usage metadata.

Existing flag attribution remains at public/assets/images/flags/SOURCES.md.
